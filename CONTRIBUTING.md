# Contributing to Proxima

Proxima is a proprietary product of Conzex Global Private Limited. We welcome
feedback and bug reports through our official support channels:

- **Bug reports:** Email support@conzex.com with details about the issue.
- **Feature requests:** Email product@conzex.com with your suggestions.
- **General inquiries:** Visit [https://www.conzex.com](https://www.conzex.com).
